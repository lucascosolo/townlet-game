## Description

Six people live in a clearing by a brook. You decide what goes up around them: a bakery, a bench, a path down to the well. They notice.

Ada wants to plant the orchard her late sister planned. Bram is a baker without an oven. Fen fishes the brook and doesn't say much. Juniper's head is full of half-built gadgets, and Wren, who lives with her, sketches everything. Marlow hasn't decided whether he's staying.

Each of them has needs, opinions and a dream of their own. Put a workshop next to a light sleeper and you'll hear about it in the morning. Build the bench someone has been asking for and they'll remember that too. Friendships form, grudges set in, and now and then two people make up.

**What you do**

- Build homes, workshops, gardens and places to gather. Lay paths and see which ways people actually walk.
- Talk to people. Ask how they are or what they think of you, and fill in your Folk album as you get to know them.
- Answer their requests, or don't. They'll tell you how they feel about it, and they'll tell each other.
- Get ready for winter. Food runs short if nobody plans ahead.
- Grow from a Clearing to a Hamlet, then a Village, then a Townlet. New neighbours move in as the town grows.

**Good to know**

- Free, and it runs in your browser on a phone or a computer.
- Your town saves itself in the browser you play in.
- Nobody dies and nothing burns down. A bad week is something you can put right, though someone who's unhappy for long enough might pack up and leave.
- It's early. Expect rough edges, and please tell me what you find.
