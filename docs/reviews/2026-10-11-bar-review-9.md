# Townlet: bar review 9 (2026-10-11)

This is the ninth independent review against the bar in `docs/GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` section 9.4, narrowed as the brief (`2026-10-11-review-9-brief.md`) asks: the four phases of the plan of 2026-10-11 as a player meets them, and one question, what there was to decide on days 15 to 30. I read spec 9.3 from "the plan of 2026-10-11" to "Phase D status" for the builder's criteria and numbers, and review 8 for its shape only. Every number, quote and screenshot here is from my own sessions. Screenshots are in `docs/reviews/shots-9/`: `p390` and `p360` are the phones (`p390-turned` after the turn), `d1280` the five-day desktop game, `d-` the thirty-day desktop game and `logprobe` the save probe.

## What I played

I served the built site in `dist/` with `vite preview` on port 4174 and drove the real UI with Playwright (the installed Chromium 1194, swiftshader). Every action a player takes went through the on-screen controls: the intro cards, the tab bar and sheet on the phones, the build tray's cards with a tap on a tile and "✓ Place" (a click on the tile with the mouse), the proposal modal and the board's "Decide…", the Journal roster, the question and reply chips, the favour buttons, the Folk album and You tab. The debug handle advanced time (`runTicks`), brought a tile on screen (`focus`) and was read (`state`, `residentScreen`, `tileScreen`) to know where a person or tile was on screen before tapping it. Seed 15 of the bakery scenario throughout.

- **Phase A, three devices, a fresh town with the intro to day 5 each.** 390×844 with touch, turned to 844×390 at 18:00 on day 2 and played sideways to day 5; 360×780 with touch; 1280×800 with a mouse. On each: three intro cards, 90 real seconds at the starting speed, then each morning the proposal if one popped, all five tabs with the HUD measured after each, the Goals tier card (phone) or tier line (desktop), a building placed at 09:00 (bench, hedge, flower bed, bench, flower bed) with rotate and cancel, and at noon two people tapped in the town (or opened from the journal on the desktop) and asked two questions each with a reply, so that everyone was asked "How are you?" by day 5. On days 2 and 5 I read all six Folk pages. Every visible control was measured on every tab, every morning.
- **Phase B, saves, in the thirty-day game.** On day 3 and day 12 at 08:05 I put the page away (navigated off it, which saves) and opened the plain URL in a new page, then compared the minute, buildings, residents, moods, asks, proposals, stock and the Town log. On day 12 I then did the brief's older-save test in the console, twice: once exactly as written, and once with the page's own saving stopped (`Storage.prototype.setItem` made a no-op) before the reload, because the first way turned out not to test what it means to. I also ran a separate probe on day 2 to look at what the board, Folk pages and You tab show before and after a reload.
- **Phase D, thirty days on the 1280×800 desktop as an engaged steward.** Each morning at 08:05 I answered proposals on a rotation (approve, approve, decline, decide later, with anything put off answered next from the board), at 08:30 granted every open ask with the building it named or the obvious one (flower bed for green, bench to sit or gather, garden for food, cottage for kin, the dream building for a dream) placed near the asker's home, at noon talked to three people with two questions each (how, mind, hope, me and "What do you think of…" in turn) and replied to each answer (sorry when offered, "What's been the best of it?" every other time, otherwise agree and disagree in turn), at 12:40 asked one favour, and on days 12 and 28 asked everyone "What do you think of me?". Each night I recorded everyone's mood and standing, the stock and the granary. From day 15 the morning board, the open asks, proposals, newcomers, dreams and anyone thinking of leaving went into a diary. On days 15, 22 and 30 I read the You tab and every Folk page.
- **Totals.** 30 days, 13 residents by the end, 202 answers and 180 replies, 30 favours asked, 12 proposals answered, 24 buildings placed.

**Crashes and console.** Nothing crashed and there were no page errors in any session. The console showed only a failed font request (the sandbox's certificate, `ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID`). My own harness fell over three times (a tray closed after a placement, the log's per-group buttons, a modal under a click); I fixed it and resumed from the saved town each time, which is itself evidence the save works.

**Load times** (slow machine, software rendering, up to three browsers on four cores): a fresh town 2.5 s to the debug handle alone, 5.7 s and 7.4 s with other sessions running; the day 3 reload 3.8 s, the day 12 reload 7.6 s, the day 30 reopen 3.4 s alone; the replay of 16,325 ticks 18.5 s.

## Harness limits

- **One favour rotation over-asked Ada.** My rotation landed on Ada on days 2 to 6 and 8, so she refused twice ("You have asked a great deal of me lately", "Favours asked this week: 5 (that is a lot)"). A real player would have spread them. I count the refusals as the game working, not as a finding.
- **Quieter nights were never answered.** My steward built only what an ask named. The "quieter nights" card says "A hedge between the noise and their home softens it", which my script did not do, so three newcomers' asks about the bakery at night stayed open from day 12. What followed (below) is the game's fair reaction to that neglect, and I report it as such.
- **Pacing at live speed** I saw only in the 90 seconds after each intro.
- **The camera** was moved with `focus`, as the board's "Show me" does.
- **On the Folk pages** the About/Talk sub-view persists per resident, so my day-30 page reads came from a reopen of the saved town with About chosen explicitly.

## Scores

### 1. Fun: 7/10

Up from 6. The second fortnight has things in it this time (the diary is below): newcomers every four days once a cottage stood, a bell, a market, a bonfire to decide, a granary that is eaten in the last week, and two people turned against me for a reason I could have fixed. Choices landed: every cottage I built brought someone (Noah, Maud, Iris, Emrys, Rosa, Silas, Gideon); every noise ask I ignored cost me a resident's regard.

It is not an 8 because the second fortnight's decisions are mostly the same two asks again and again, there is no dream to help after day 7, and the dreams converge as they did in review 8: on day 30 Juniper, Marlow and Noah all held "share the commons with a friend". Timber sat at the 100 cap from day 25 with nothing I wanted.

### 2. Polished: 7/10

The phone work holds up at the screen: on both phones, upright and turned, the clock, stock and renown were in view and uncovered after every tab on every morning, nothing scrolled sideways (scrollWidth 390, 360 and 844 throughout), a building was placed, turned and cancelled by touch every day, a person was tapped in the town every day, and the reply chips were 44 px. The desktop Goals widget shows its three goals and the tier line whole at 1280×800, and the tier line opens the track.

It stays at 7 because: a reload empties the Town log (see Phase B); the intro's Next and Begin, the proposal modal's Approve and Decline, and the board's "N more in the Town log" links are 31, 31 and 17 px tall on the phones; the proposal modal covers the stock and renown on a sideways phone; and the talk panel still carries raw joins ("Kind of, you've made this place feel like home.", "As I told you on day 28:" about something said that morning, "I can't abide a busy lane" as a tag on four unrelated answers).

### 3. Thinking sims: 7/10

Pages explain what people do. Maud's page on day 30 reads "Whether to leave (thinking of leaving since day 25)", "Wants some quiet (too much company for their liking)", "The first bakery keeps her up at night", "Maybe the steward doesn't think things through…", and she had told me "Oh, I don't think you care about us at all. Thing is, you build without thinking." Emrys, with the same grievance: "Oh, it is YOU." Ada's You-tab entry "▼ Day 30: put the bakery there" matches her page's "annoyance about the bakery by Gideon's cottage", which I had built two days before.

It is not an 8 because the same page can contradict itself. Maud "Loves a bit of bustle about the place" three lines above "Wants some quiet (too much company for their liking)" and "annoyance about the commons". Ada feels "joy about the flower bed, annoyance about the flower bed" at once. Fen is "Cross about the bench" with no reason on the page. Juniper is "Missing Noah (hasn't seen Noah since day 28)" on day 30.

### 4. Opinionated sims: 7/10

Two newcomers turned on me and stayed turned: Maud's standing fell from +0.03 (day 7) to −0.24 (day 14), −0.44 (day 22) and −0.99 (day 30); she refused favours three times ("I'm sorry, I don't feel I can, the way things are.") and has been thinking of leaving since day 25. Emrys fell to −0.55. Proposals split the town by name every time. Ada would not be gracious to Bram ("I am not ready to be gracious to Bram.") and then made peace with Juniper as a dream of her own.

It stays at 7 because the founders all "think the world of you" by day 30 (standings 0.59 to 0.83), 18 of 20 sorries were taken, and three people shared one dream.

### 5. Expressive and interactive: 7/10

202 answers, 180 replies, 130 distinct responses, none more than four times. The voices hold: Bram "Rude! But yes!", Emrys "Crickets! Absolute crickets!", Maud "It's all right. I'm used to waiting." then "Never mind. I'll manage without.", Juniper "I keep it like a pressed flower." "What's been the best of it?" brings back real things ("you planted my sister's orchard", "I got a cottage for my cousin", "the day Wren's banner went up").

It stays at 7 because "best of it" leans on one shape ("you listened, and I got …" in 10 of 26), a stock clause still tags answers ("I can't abide a busy lane" closed four "What's on your mind?" answers about other things), "As I told you on day 28:" prefixed a line said the same morning, and the tics land before the wrong sentences ("Kind of, you've made this place feel like home.", "Like, ask me next week!").

## Verdict

**The game does not meet the bar.** No count reaches 8: Fun 7, Polished 7, Thinking 7, Opinionated 7, Expressive 7.

Against the plan of 2026-10-11, at the screen:
- **Phase A** holds for the layouts, the HUD, the sideways phone, the desktop Goals widget and the "loves" wording. It does not hold for criterion 3 (44 px) on the intro, the proposal modal and the board's log links, which the builder's test never measures because it opens neither.
- **Phase B** holds for the minute, the buildings, the residents, their moods, the asks and the proposals, and the older-save replay works. It does not hold for the Town log, which is empty after every reload from a snapshot; the brief's literal console test cannot run on this build because the page writes a fresh snapshot as it is put away.
- **Phase D criterion 6** holds as a count: 19 things to decide on days 15 to 28 against the spec's 16 to 21. What the count hides is that 8 of the 19 were the same two asks repeated, none was a dream, and the fortnight's real pressure (the larder at zero, the granary feeding the town) was a Worries card, not an ask.

## Problems, ranked by how much they hold the game back

### 1. A reload empties the Town log and the morning's news (Phase B)

What I saw:
- Day 2 probe: before the reload the log held 9, 21 and 51 rows on Highlights, Story and Everything (`logprobe-before`); after the plain URL came back from the snapshot it held 0, 0 and 0 (`logprobe-after`), and after an hour only the one line made since ("10:04 Marlow muses: 'What a lovely day it has been.'"). The board's "This morning" read "Nothing new overnight." though the morning before had three asks on it.
- Day 3 and day 12 of the real game: the same, 23 and 169 collapsed groups on Everything before, 0 after (`d-d3-after-reload`, `d-d12-after-reload`).
- The replay path keeps it: with the save stripped to commands the day 12 town came back by replay with 174 groups in the log (`d-d12-after-replay`). So this is new with the snapshot, and review 8's "the log full (514 rows)" was the replay.
- Everything held in the sim state came back whole: the same minute (3365, 16325), the same 48 and 55 buildings, the same 7 and 9 residents with the same moods, the same asks, proposals, stock and wishes, the Folk pages with their facts and memories, the You tab, the goals. The town then carried on without error.

Where: the log and the morning list are collected by the UI from events as the sim emits them; a snapshot restore emits none of them (`loaded === 'snapshot'`, `web/src/game.ts` and the log pane in `web/src/ui/ui.ts`, `buildLogPane` around line 1358). The spec's Phase B criteria never name the log, so the tests pass.

Fix: put the log (or the last few days of it) in the save beside the snapshot, or keep it in the sim state so the snapshot carries it; keep yesterday's morning list the same way; add "the Town log is intact" to Phase B criterion 2.

### 2. The second fortnight is full, but of the same two asks (Phase D criterion 6)

What I saw, the diary of days 15 to 30 (asks are listed the morning they first appeared; "quieter" is "asks for quieter nights" about the bakery; standing is the asker's affinity for me that night):
- **Day 15.** Emrys moved into the cottage. Bram proposed a night of tales (put off, then approved from the board). Bram asked a third time for a cottage for his cousin; built. Maud's quieter-nights ask still open. Favour: Wren cut timber. Hamlet; stores 126/210.
- **Day 16.** Emrys asked for a woodlot; built. Nothing new overnight otherwise.
- **Day 17.** The same open asks; nothing new. The town became a Village (renown 285).
- **Day 18.** Emrys proposed a weekly market; approved. Nothing asked.
- **Day 19.** Rosa moved in. Bram asked a fourth time for a cottage; built. Maud: "It's all right. I'm used to waiting."
- **Day 20.** Emrys asked for quieter nights; left open. "Wish granted: quieter nights" on the board, though three people still asked.
- **Day 21.** Iris proposed a bonfire; declined. Maud asked again for quieter nights.
- **Day 22.** Winter stores met (210). The larder fell to 14 and the Worries card began: "The larder is nearly bare, and the granary is feeding the town: 281 food left in it."
- **Day 23.** Silas moved in. Bram asked a fifth time for a cottage; built. Larder 3, granary 260.
- **Day 24.** Ada proposed a bell for quiet at ten (put off, then approved). Silas asked for a garden; built. Larder 9.
- **Day 25.** Larder 0, granary 245. Maud began thinking of leaving; she refused a favour.
- **Day 26.** "Maud is thinking of leaving. 'I keep thinking about leaving. I hate that I do.'" on the board. Larder 1, granary 218.
- **Day 27.** Gideon moved in. Bram asked a sixth time for a cottage; built. Noah asked for quieter nights. Larder 0, granary 198.
- **Day 28.** Gideon asked for a bakery; built (Ada minded). Maud: "Never mind. I'll manage without." Larder 1, granary 176.
- **Day 29.** Year 1; stores target 390. "The season ended without quieter nights. Maud and Emrys had hoped for it." Emrys asked again.
- **Day 30.** Emrys asked again; Maud still thinking of leaving; timber 100 since day 25.

Counted the spec's way for days 15 to 28: 4 proposals, 10 asks, 4 arrivals, 1 leaving warning, 0 dream steps asked of me, 0 stores asks: 19, inside the spec's 16 to 21. The first fortnight on the same town held about 20 (4 proposals, 13 asks of which 3 were dreams, 3 arrivals). So the second fortnight was as full as the first, and some of it mattered: four cottages became four people, the ignored noise asks turned Maud and Emrys against me, and the larder emptied. But of the 10 asks, 4 were Bram's cottage again and 4 were the bakery's noise again; no dream asked anything of me after Wren's banner on day 7; and the larder was never an ask, only a worry.

Where: cottage asks recur from `home_for_kin` whenever the town may grow and no home is empty (`src/sim/asks.ts`); the noise asks lapse after five days and are re-posted; dreams after the authored first ones are templates (`share`, `gift`, `thank`, `mend`, `place`) that ask nothing of the steward; `more_food` wants a shortage day, which the granary prevents.

Fix: vary what growth asks for (a workshop for Emrys's trade, a second jetty, a teahouse once a dozen live here) instead of the same cottage; let one of the template dreams need the steward (a bench to remember by, a path to a place); make the granary drawing down an ask from whoever keeps it, with a number ("the granary will last nine days").

### 3. Buttons under 44 px on the phones, where the builder's test does not look (Phase A criterion 3)

What I saw, on 390×844 and 360×780 upright and on 844×390:
- The intro's Next and Begin: 31 px tall, 63 to 72 px wide (`p390-intro1`, `p390-intro3`).
- The proposal modal's Approve and Decline: 31 px; "Decide later" is a text link (`p390-d2-modal`, `p390-turned-d3-modal`).
- The board's "6 more in the Town log" links: 17 px (every morning on the Goals sheet).
- Everything else measured 44 px or more on every tab, every morning (tab bar 53 px, chips 44, place bar buttons 44, questions 44).
- Sideways, the proposal card sits over the stock and renown numbers (`p390-turned-d3-modal`); upright the backdrop dims the HUD but leaves it readable.

Where: `e2e/devices.spec.ts` measures controls after the intro has been pressed through and never opens a proposal, so the three smallest controls a new player meets are outside it; `.intro` and `.modal` buttons in `web/src/style.css`.

Fix: give the intro and modal buttons the phone's 44 px height and make "Decide later" a button; make the "more" links buttons; measure the intro and a modal in the device test.

### 4. One page, two minds (and "loves" that are relative)

What I saw:
- Maud, day 30: "Loves a bit of bustle about the place" and, on the same page, "Wants some quiet (too much company for their liking)" and "annoyance about the commons" (`d-d30-about-nc15-1-vmshmn`). Emrys the same: "Loves a bit of bustle about the place" beside "Maybe the commons isn't his sort of place…".
- Ada: "joy about the flower bed, annoyance about the flower bed".
- Fen: "Cross about the bench (annoyance about the bench)" with no cause, three days after I put a bench near Ada.
- Rosa, Silas and Gideon all "Wants some quiet (too much company for their liking)" in a town of 13 that socialises at one commons.

The "loves" lines. Every one I saw, on the phones' toasts and Folk pages and on the day-30 desktop pages:
- Ada: "Loves green all round the house" (and in talk: "Nothing lifts me like green all round the house."). Her green liking is 0.76, her strongest: earned.
- Bram: "Loves a lane with people in it" ("Give me a lane with people in it and I'm happy."). Bustle 0.52: earned.
- Fen: "Loves a garden to look at" ("I'm never better than with a garden to look at."). Green 0.83, his strongest, though "a garden to look at" is an odd thing for the fisherman to love most; it is the wording drawn for him, not his taste.
- Juniper: "Loves people passing the door" ("People passing the door always cheers me."). Bustle 0.28: earned, barely.
- Marlow: "Loves company in the lane". Bustle 0.57: earned.
- Wren: "Loves the smell of baking and blossom" ("There's no better tonic for me than the smell of baking and blossom."). Scent 0.56: earned.
- Maud: "Loves a bit of bustle about the place". Her bustle liking is 0.25; she likes green twice as much (0.55) and water more (0.40). She gets bustle because it is what she likes more than the rest of the cast, not what she likes most, and her page says she wants quiet.
- Emrys: "Loves a bit of bustle about the place". Bustle 0.38, his strongest: earned.
- Noah, Iris, Rosa, Silas and Gideon had not yet shown theirs.

So criterion 7's letter holds (nobody loves a quality under 0.15; bustle is said four ways), and the one that reads unearned is Maud's, where the relative rule picks a weak liking over her real one.

Where: `factValue('lifts')` in `src/sim/progress.ts` ranks by liking minus the cast mean, so a town of sociable newcomers makes a mild liking for company "what she loves". The need line comes from `needs.company` over its setpoint.

Fix: require the relative winner to be at least, say, two thirds of the absolute top, else say the absolute top; never say "loves bustle" while "wants some quiet" is on the page; drop a forming belief when its opposite is settled (the flower bed).

### 5. Raw joins in the talk panel

What I saw:
- "I can't abide a busy lane." closing Wren's, Ada's and Fen's "What's on your mind?" answers about a quiet corner, Bram, and Marlow's cart (days 2, 4, 5, 8). Criterion 7 fixed the loves; the dislike still says the one phrase.
- "As I told you on day 28: You? Best steward going!" (Noah, asked twice on day 28).
- "Kind of, you've made this place feel like home." (Wren), "Like, ask me next week!" (Noah), "Like, my heart is full, honestly." (Wren).
- "I am very well indeed, thank you. Honestly, I am grateful to you. I should have said so sooner. I work at the garden plots." (Ada, day 1: four sentences from four sources).
- "Honestly, a little conversation would not go amiss. I must say, patience. I need to make something for Fen, in the evenings at home." (Ada, day 18).
- Names for repeats: "the first bakery", "the second bakery", "the old bakery" and "the bakery by Gideon's cottage" for two buildings; "the old woodlot" and "the new woodlot".

Where: `src/narrate/narrator.ts` (the tag clause and the "As I told you" prefix), `src/content/voice.ts` (the tics).

Fix: give the dislike the same several wordings the loves got; prefix "As I told you" only to hopes and views, never to a line said today; keep one tic per answer and none before "you've"; name repeat buildings by their place from the first repeat.

### 6. Sideways is usable, but tight

What I saw on 844×390: the town band is about 220 px between the HUD and the rolled sheet (`p390-turned-town`); with the place bar up it is about 150 px (`p390-turned-d3-build-flowerbed-outline`); a sheet fills the band so the answer scrolls out of view the moment the questions show (`p390-turned-d3-talk-marlow`). Everything the brief asks worked: the layout stayed the phone's, each tab left the HUD clear, a person could be tapped in the town, a flower bed and a bench were placed, turned and cancelled.

Fix: on a sideways phone, let the HUD shrink to one row of icons and the place bar sit at the side.

## What I could not check

- **Real phones** (criterion 9) and WebKit: emulation on Chromium only, as the brief allows.
- **Pacing at live speed** beyond 90 seconds per device.
- **The brief's literal older-save test.** Editing `townlet.save.v1` in the console and reloading cannot produce a replay on this build, because the page saves again as it goes away (`pagehide`) and overwrote my edit with a fresh version-2 snapshot; the reload came back from the snapshot at the same minute. With the page's saving stopped, the same edit came back by replay (`loaded: 'replay'`) at the same minute with the same 55 buildings, the same nine residents and the same asks, in 18.5 s. I report both; a save written by an older build would meet the second case.
- **Hedges against noise.** My steward never built one, so I cannot say whether a hedge would have quieted Maud's and Emrys's nights.
- **A year's save size and the fixtures:** headless claims I did not re-run.
