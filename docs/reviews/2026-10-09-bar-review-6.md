# Townlet: bar review 6 (2026-10-09)

This is the sixth independent review against the bar in `docs/GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` section 9.4: five counts, each scored out of 10, with 8 as the pass mark on every one. I read reviews 1 to 5 only to learn what had been claimed fixed and to match their shape. Every number, quote and screenshot here comes from my own sessions. Screenshots are in `docs/reviews/shots-6/`: `p` is the phone, `d` the engaged desktop game and `n` the neglected desktop game.

## What I played

I built the game with `npm run build`, served it with `vite preview` on port 4173, and drove the real UI with Playwright (chromium, swiftshader). Everything went through the on-screen controls: the Build tray and a click on a tile, the Remove tool, the proposal modal, the Journal roster, and the question, reply, picker and favour chips. I placed nothing by command.

- **Engaged, 30 days, desktop 1440x900, `bakery` seed 3.** I chose a seed no earlier review used. Each morning at 08:05 I read the board and answered proposals on a fixed rotation as they came up. That was 9 modals: 5 approved, 3 declined and 1 "Decide later". The one I put off came back after my reload and I approved it.
  - **Building.** I granted building asks near the asker's home and followed a building plan. 21 buildings went up: two bakeries, garden plots, an orchard (Ada's dream), hedges, flower beds, two banners, cottages, a bench and a workshop. 16 more attempts were refused for lack of timber, all between days 3 and 14.
  - **Talking.** Each noon I talked to three residents, two questions each, rotating all five questions ("What do you think of…" alternated between people and places). After each answer I replied, favouring sorry or explain whenever they were offered. Every ninth answer I left without a reply. That came to 186 answers and 166 replies: 72 agree, 79 disagree, 10 sorry and 5 explain, with both hope chips used.
  - **The "me" round.** On day 13 I asked everyone "What do you think of me?" and apologised wherever I could.
  - **Favours.** I asked 33 favours, including four timber requests to Marlow in a row on day 17.
  - **Save and reload.** On day 10 I saved and reopened the plain URL.
  - **Removing a loved place.** On day 14 I picked the place residents' own beliefs held most dear, the commons (loved by Bram, Marlow, Wren and Hollis), and removed it with the Remove tool. On days 15 and 16 I talked to the people who had loved it.
  - **The larder.** I put off Juniper's granary throughout. From day 18 to day 26 I built no food. On day 19 I removed five of the six food places, including Ada's orchard, the jetty and both bakeries. I rebuilt two bakeries and an orchard on day 27.
  - **Records and the day-30 survey.** I recorded mood, food and every resident's standing each night. On day 30 I read the board, the You tab, all eight About pages, the Goals and Folk widgets, and the log in all three filters.
  - **Outcome.** The town grew from 6 to 8 (Hollis and Agnes), became a Hamlet on day 6 and a Village on day 24, and ended with 358 renown.
- **Neglected, 21 days, desktop, `quiet` seed 5.** I never built, talked or answered. The six proposal modals got "Decide later" because they block the screen. I read the board on days 1, 7, 14 and 21 and the You tab on day 21. On day 22 I read four About pages and asked Ada, Fen, Juniper and Marlow how, me, mind and hope, replying sorry, or disagree where sorry wasn't offered.
- **Fresh on a phone, 390x844 touch, `bakery` seed 5, with the intro.**
  - I went through the three intro cards and tapped Log straight after Begin.
  - Then I let 90 real seconds run and tapped all five nav tabs.
  - I opened Ada from the Folk album: About, then Talk with four questions and a reply to each.
  - I tapped Bram on the map for the quick card, and placed a flower bed with the touch outline and "✓ Place".
- **Headless.** I ran `npm run radio` for `bakery` seed 8 (30 days, considerate), `quiet` seed 6 (30 days, no steward flag) and `quiet` seed 11 (30 days, favours), and `npm run inspect` for `bakery` seed 8 at day 14.

**Crashes and console.** Nothing crashed, and there were no page errors in any session. The console showed only three things:
- The three.js warning "PCFSoftShadowMap has been removed. Using PCFShadowMap instead."
- A failed request for the Google font (ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID). That is the sandbox proxy, not the game, so the page fell back to a system typeface.
- The matching "Failed to load resource" console error.

Save and reload worked: the game came back at the same tick (13860 to 13861) with the same 54 buildings and 8 residents, the log full (288 rows) and no intro. At 390 px the page never scrolled sideways (scrollWidth 390 on all eight checks).

**Harness limits.**
- **Time.** Software rendering runs at 1 to 2 fps, so I advanced time with `runTicks` between actions rather than watching it play. I read bubbles and toasts at moments I chose.
- **Timings.** The three browser sessions ran side by side on four cores for the first half hour. The phone's load times (handle at 6.7 s, loader gone at 9.3 s) and the 11.1 s reload are slower than a real device would see, so I do not score them.
- **Fixed rotations.** Questions, replies and building spots followed a rotation, not taste. Putting off Juniper's granary for 23 days was a deliberate test, but a real player would probably have built it.
- **`new=1` overrides `seed`.** It starts a random town even when the URL names a seed (`web/src/main.ts` line 32). I opened named seeds in fresh browser contexts without `new=1` and confirmed `state.seed`.
- **Live play.** On the phone, 90 seconds of live play reached only 09:53 on day 1.

## Scores

### 1. Fun: 7/10

Review 5's main complaint was that by mid-month nothing you did moved the town. That is fixed: this month had consequences.
- **The larder bit.** Mean mood held at 0.71 to 0.79 for the first 22 nights. Once the larder emptied it fell to 0.64 (day 23), 0.56, 0.52 and 0.48 (day 26), and stayed near 0.5 to day 30.
  - The board filled with "WORRIES: The larder is empty, and everyone knows it." and asks from all eight people ("Juniper asks you: 'My stomach has opinions about the larder.'", d25-board).
  - Favours dried up: "I am afraid I cannot work on an empty stomach.", "Feed us first. Then ask."
- **Standing is earned and lost.** On day 18 the founders stood at Ada 0.69, Bram 0.67, Fen 0.40, Juniper 0.02, Marlow 0.26 and Wren 0.87. By day 30 they were at 0.07, −0.13, −0.36, −0.95, 0.05 and −0.19.
  - Juniper, whose granary I kept putting off, was "thinking of leaving" and told me "Hmm, nobody is steering this place."
- **Losing the commons rippled for days.** Bram: "I cried about the commons! Yes, I did!" Hollis: "You know, I keep turning to where the commons was." Two days later Marlow "has decided the town isn't the same without the commons". On day 29 Bram was still saying "We lost the commons! Don't think I've forgotten!"
- **Over-asking still has a cost.** Marlow went from "I'm still on the last thing you asked!" (three times on day 17) to "I've done a lot for you this week. I need a break." on day 20.
- **Neglect bites.** In the neglected town mood went from 0.72 to 0.55, Bram left on day 19, and Juniper and Wren were thinking of leaving by day 21. Ada gave up: "The steward has made their position clear enough."
- **The town surprises.** A travelling fiddler, Fen's birthday ("Ada and Wren remembered."), Ada leaving "a parcel of food and timber on your step", and people arguing about me ("You're too hard on the steward, Juniper. Honestly.").

It stays at 7 because the first hour and the economy fight the player:
- **The first two weeks are a timber famine.** 16 builds were refused between days 3 and 14 ("Not enough timber for a glasshouse (14 needed)." eight days running). Juniper's glasshouse never got built. By day 19 timber had piled up to 48, and to 63 by day 22, with nothing I wanted to spend it on.
- **Answering a food ask doesn't count in winter.** On day 27 I built two bakeries and an orchard. The food asks lapsed anyway within two days ("Fen: 'Asked the steward. Might as well not have.'", "Marlow: 'I suppose nobody heard me, then.'"). Every resident took "kept me waiting 5 days for a fuller larder" onto their You-tab entry. A player who did respond is punished as if they hadn't.
- **Proposals recur within the month.** The tales night came twice, the lantern walk twice and Pip's cart stop twice. Each repeat came with the same pitch, word for word ("Pip wants to stop the night with the cart…").

### 3. Thinking sims: 7/10

You can find out why people act, and most of the reasons hold up:
- **Hunger flows through everything.** It shows in "how" answers ("We'll be sharing crusts soon.", "Who ate everything? Was it me?", "The shelves are bare and echoing."), in favours refused for hunger, and in the board's worry. It also feeds the You tab's "let the larder run bare" on six people.
- **Loss works when the attachment is real.** I removed the commons because, in their beliefs, Bram, Marlow, Wren and Hollis held it dear.
  - All four reacted that day.
  - Bram and Marlow raised it unprompted for two more days ("You took away the commons! I am still cross!").
  - Hollis listed "The commons was where the good evenings happen." among his settled opinions on day 30.
- **"You don't seem it" now reads the person.** 11 of 12 people I doubted were in good spirits (mood 0.70 to 0.87) and denied it ("No no no, I'm great! Look at me!"). The one who conceded was at 0.697.
- **Plans move and finish.** Ada went from her sister's orchard to "make peace with Juniper". In the neglected town Ada and Juniper "made it up", and both remembered it on their pages.

It loses points where minds contradict themselves or miss what matters most:
- **Ada didn't grieve her own dream.** On day 19 I removed the orchard I had planted for her as her sister's wish, which she called "It meant a great deal". All she said was "The view is rather the poorer for it." The next day she was "very well indeed", and two days later she left me a gift. Her day-30 page still leads with "you planted my sister's orchard". The removal reaction only fires on beliefs about the building (`src/sim/sim.ts` around lines 850 to 857), and nothing gives a dreamer a belief about the building their dream produced.
- **Opinions that cancel out.** Hollis's settled opinions on day 30 include both "The teahouse isn't his sort of place." and "The teahouse is where the good evenings happen.", with "Favourite spot: the teahouse" above them.
- **Answers that contradict the page or the bubble.**
  - On the phone, three minutes in, Ada's bubble said "Frankly, the steward still irritates me." and her page said "Cross about the steward". Her answer to "What do you think of me?" a moment later was "I think well of you, steward."
  - Marlow said "I don't really know Fen yet. I keep thinking about how Fen and I made it up, six days ago."
  - Fen's settled opinions on day 30 still say "The steward makes wishes come true." at −0.36 standing, under "doesn't trust you".
- **Praise outruns the standing behind it.** Ada at 0.41 standing, the morning after I let the larder run bare, said "I could not ask for a better steward." At 0.34 she said "You have been a true credit to this valley." The top "love" band starts at 0.3 (`src/sim/talk.ts` line 23), and standing rests at 0.15.
- **Some first steps still stick.** Marlow's "watch the trade cart come and go" was his bubble on days 1, 2, 3 and 4. Fen's "Find someone willing to learn" ran days 2 and 3, and their dream then finished with no named pupil (the "someone" line above).

### 4. Opinionated sims: 8/10

This is the count that moved most. Residents now hold things against you that you cannot talk away:
- **They think ill of you, in their own words.** Of 44 "What do you think of me?" answers in the engaged town, 5 were plainly negative:
  - Juniper: "Hmm, your hands are elsewhere.", "Ooh, your hands are elsewhere. Somehow, you let the larder run bare.", "Hmm, nobody is steering this place."
  - Fen: "Could be better, couldn't it. You don't listen."
  - Wren: "I wish you'd listen a bit more. Well, you make poor decisions."

  Many of the kind ones carried a real grievance: "You're a marvel, you know that? … Except you kept me waiting 2 days for proper places to work, still cross!"
- **The neglected town turned on me.**
  - Ada: "You have disappointed me, if I may say so. You see, you don't listen."
  - Marlow: "I'm not sure you care about us, honestly. Well, you make poor decisions."
  - Juniper: "The valley does not feel yours anymore."
- **Apologies can fail.** Juniper at −0.56 in the neglected town answered "Too late, and too light.", then "I heard it the first time. Let it settle." An explanation to Wren at −0.17 got "I'm sure you had your reasons. I'm still hurt, though."
- **Neighbours and places divide them:**
  - "Rather keep clear of Bram." (Fen, day 1)
  - "Bram and I do not see eye to eye." (Ada)
  - "Ada? Sore point! I mean, she is hard to get along with!" (Bram)
  - "There is a draught between Fen and me." (Juniper)
  - "I have nothing good to say about the teahouse." (Ada)
  - Quarrels ran all month ("Juniper is not getting on with Marlow.", "Ada and I are two stones that will not sit together.").
- **Decisions split the town by taste.** Every proposal named who was for and against. Each one landed on both sides in the bubbles: "☁ Thinks less of you: said yes to Bram's bonfire night" (Ada) the same morning as "♥ Thinks better of you: said yes to Bram's bonfire night" (Wren).

It is an 8, not higher:
- **Sorry still lands with people who distrust you if the grievance is fresh.** Juniper at −0.58 standing and 0.08 trust: "Something unclenches. Thank you."
- **Explain is accepted for things that were never decisions.** "Let me explain why I never got him a fuller larder." drew "Oh. I wish I'd known. That makes sense." from Marlow, and Fen took the same chip for jobs ("All right, that makes sense."). This is because `ignores_me` is in `DECISION_GRIEVANCES` (`src/sim/replies.ts` line 66).
- **Tastes still converge a little.** "Loves growing things" is on Ada and Fen, and "Loves a busy lane" on Juniper and Hollis. "Not much bothers me, truly." closed 5 answers.

### 5. Expressive and interactive: 8/10

Talking back now lands by what you said and to whom:
- **The chips answer the answer.** Mood answers offer "Good. I'm glad." / "You don't seem it." when well and "That's hard. I'm listening." / "It can't be all bad." when not. Hope answers offer "I hope it comes true." / "Is that really what you want?", or "Take your time." / "Don't settle for too little." between dreams. Sorry and explain name the grievance that was actually voiced ("I'm sorry I took away the commons.").
- **Pushing back between dreams has its own answers now:** "Not settling. Resting." (Fen), "Fallow fields grow the best crops." (Juniper), "A fair challenge. Something larger will come to me." (Ada), "That's sweet. I'll dream something big, promise." (Wren).
- **Responses vary and stay in character.** 121 of 166 were distinct, and none came more than four times.
  - Bram, disagreed with: "Ooh, a debate! Wrong, but a debate!", "Agree to disagree! Loudly!", "Well, excuse ME!"
  - Ada: "Interesting. I remain unpersuaded.", "Allow me my opinion, steward."
  - Juniper: "We see different weather, you and I.", "Then let it be two things at once."
- **The voices are a pleasure to read:** "I counted the larder twice. It did not improve.", "I planted a wish and now I wait for rain.", "Juniper: 'I put a wish on the board and the board kept it.'", "Something went sour between me and Ada."

It is an 8, not higher:
- **The third-person grievance appears inside the conversation itself.** You read it in the chip, and the resident says it ("I'm sorry I never got her more green about the place.").
- **Answers are long.** 56 of 186 ran to four or five sentences, often ending on a stock line ("Not much bothers me, truly." 5 times, "I have no odd habits to speak of." 3 times, "I work at the garden plots." 3 times).
- **Tics still glue onto answer openings:** "Kind of, the woodlot? I haven't made my mind up.", "Like, I think the world of you, you know."
- **Praise for the steward reuses the same clauses:** "Well, you listen." 7 times and "Well, you make wishes come true." 5 times.

## Verdict

**The game does not meet the bar.** Opinionated and Expressive reach 8. Fun, Polished and Thinking are at 7.

Round 5 fixed what review 5 ranked first and second:
- Standing is now earned by deeds and lost by neglect.
- A famine bites.
- Replies land by what was said and by standing.
- The phone log is there from the first look.
- The board no longer claims nothing needs you.

What holds it under now is this:
- An economy that starves the first two weeks and can't credit a winter rescue.
- Raw text in the grievances that every apology and You-tab line is built on.
- Minds that sometimes contradict themselves or ignore what mattered most to them.
- A desktop Folk widget still squeezed out for much of the year.

## Problems, ranked by how much they hold the game back

### 1. Economy pacing: a timber famine early, then a food ask you cannot answer in winter

What I saw:
- 16 builds refused for timber on days 3 to 14, while Juniper's glasshouse waited eight mornings. Timber then sat at 48 to 63 from day 19 to day 26.
- After a famine I caused, I rebuilt two bakeries and an orchard on day 27. The `more_food` asks lapsed anyway on days 28 and 29.
- That lapse left the "never got him a fuller larder" grievance and "kept me waiting 5 days for a fuller larder" on all eight residents, and "nobody heard me" on the board.

Where:
- `src/sim/asks.ts`: `more_food` is met only when `stock.food >= 15` and food was built since the ask (around lines 79 to 82).
- `ASK_LAPSE_DAYS.more_food` is 4 (line 19).

Fix:
- Count building a food place (or a favour for food) as answering a food ask, and let the larder level decide only whether the worry stays.
- Give `more_food` the same lapse as other asks.
- Give the first week a steady timber trickle, or a board hint pointing at the timber favour.

### 2. Grievance notes in the third person, in residents' own mouths, the chips and the You tab

What I saw:
- "you never got him a fuller larder" (Hollis, about himself)
- "The steward never got them proper places to work." (Fen)
- "I'm sorry I never got her more green about the place." (sorry chip)
- "kept me waiting 5 days for a fuller larder; never got him a fuller larder" (You tab)

Where:
- `src/sim/sim.ts` line 1279 writes the note as `never got ${def.pronouns.obj} …`.
- `ownNote` in `src/sim/replies.ts` (around line 145) swaps only my/me/myself.

Fix:
- Store the note in the first person ("never got me a fuller larder").
- Derive the third-person form only where the narrator reports it ("Ada thinks less of you: never got her…").

### 3. Minds that contradict themselves, and praise that outruns standing

What I saw:
- Hollis held both "The teahouse isn't his sort of place." and "The teahouse is where the good evenings happen."
- Ada's bubble said "the steward still irritates me" a minute before her answer "I think well of you, steward."
- Marlow said "I don't really know Fen yet" and "Fen and I made it up" in one answer.
- Fen's settled "The steward makes wishes come true." stood at −0.36 standing.
- Ada said "I could not ask for a better steward." at 0.41 standing during a famine.

Where:
- The `love` band at `v > 0.3` (`src/sim/talk.ts` line 23).
- The opposing place beliefs (not_for_me versus good_times), which can both settle on one subject.

Fix:
- Raise the top praise band to about 0.6, and keep it out of reach while a fresh grievance is open.
- When a belief settles, retire its opposite on the same subject.
- Let the "me" answer take an active annoyance about the steward into account.
- Skip "don't really know X" when there is a shared memory with X.

### 4. A dreamer doesn't grieve the building their dream produced

What I saw: removing Ada's sister's orchard drew only "The view is rather the poorer for it.", while she was "very well indeed" the next day and left me a gift the day after that.

Where: the removal reaction fires only on `attachment(r, b:id) > 0.15` (`src/sim/sim.ts` around lines 850 to 857). Nothing gives a dreamer a belief about the building their dream produced.

Fix: when a dream step is fulfilled by a building, seed a strong positive belief about that building. Treat removing it as a lost place with extra weight ("you took away my sister's orchard").

### 5. The Folk widget is squeezed out at 1440x900 whenever Winter stores shows

What I saw: only the header and the top 30 px of the cards, on seven desktop screenshots from day 14 onward (d14, d15, d22, d25, d27, n21, n22).

Where: the left column in `web/src/ui/ui.ts` (the widgets around line 625). The round-5 test in `e2e/round5.spec.ts` checks a different seed and day.

Fix:
- Give Folk a minimum height of one whole card row.
- Or collapse the Winter stores card to one line when the column is short.
- Test on the engaged town at days 14 to 28.

### 6. Raw and broken lines

What I saw, with where each one comes from:
- "someone has learned a great deal." (`src/narrate/narrator.ts` line 747 falls back to lowercase "someone" for a missing partner)
- "Juniper asks for help with a dream: a granary" under "'Make something for Wren.'": the stores ask in `src/sim/stores.ts` line 159 is posted as an `aspiration` with no `dream`, so `web/src/ui/ui.ts` line 1535 quotes whatever Juniper currently dreams
- "I keep picturing it." and "I want to leave it on your step." with no "it"
- Fen's teaching dream finishing with no pupil named

Fix:
- Give the stores ask its own kind and hint ("Juniper's worry: winter stores").
- Name the pupil, or say "a young one from the valley".
- Make the "it" lines name their object.

### 7. A decision modal greets a new player before the town

What I saw: on the phone, the first screen after Begin was Marlow's market proposal (07:32, day 1), naming five people the player has not met.

Where: the intro runs 7.5 hours before the UI (`web/src/main.ts` line 47), so the day-1 proposal is already waiting.

Fix: hold the first proposal until the player has had a minute with the town, or until their first tap on the map.

### 8. Repetition that wears over a month

What I saw:
- Proposals recurring word for word (the tales night, lantern walk and cart stop twice each in 30 days).
- Stuck first steps ("watch the trade cart come and go" in Marlow's bubbles for four days).
- 56 of 186 answers at four or five sentences, with stock closers.
- "Well, you listen." 7 times.
- Tics opening answers ("Kind of, the woodlot?").

Fix:
- Rest a proposal type for a season after it is answered.
- Let a first step complete after a few days or a visit.
- Cap answers at three sentences, and drop the stock closer when the answer already has a feeling or a memory.
- Keep tics off answers that open with a quoted subject.

### 9. Explain lands for things that were never decisions

What I saw: "Let me explain why I never got him a fuller larder." drew "Oh. I wish I'd known. That makes sense."

Where: `ignores_me` in `DECISION_GRIEVANCES` (`src/sim/replies.ts` line 66).

Fix: offer explain only for proposals said yes or no to. For a neglected ask, offer sorry or a promise.

### 10. Worn ground

What I saw: a large brown slab across the centre of both towns, including the one nobody built in (n21-board, d25-board).

Fix: fade wear faster where nobody builds, and cap it at path-width strips.
