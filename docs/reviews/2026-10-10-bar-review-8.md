# Townlet: bar review 8 (2026-10-10)

This is the eighth independent review against the bar in `docs/GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` section 9.4: five counts, each scored out of 10, with 8 as the pass mark on every one. I read review 7 only for its shape, and spec 9.3 only to learn what round 7 claims to have fixed. Every number, quote and screenshot here comes from my own sessions. Screenshots are in `docs/reviews/shots-8/`: `p` is the phone, `d` the engaged desktop game and `n` the neglected desktop game.

## What I played

I built the game with `npm run build`, served it with `vite preview` on port 4174, and drove the real UI with Playwright (chromium, swiftshader). Everything a player does went through on-screen controls: the intro cards, the Build tray and a click on a tile (a tap and "✓ Place" on the phone), the Remove tool, the proposal modal and the board's "Decide…" button, the Journal roster, and the question, reply, picker and favour chips. I placed and commanded nothing through the debug handle. I used seeds no earlier review used: `bakery` 15, `quiet` 16, `bakery` 17 and `bakery` 20.

- **Engaged, 30 days, desktop 1440x900, `bakery` seed 15, with the intro.**
  - **Mornings.** At 08:05 I read the board and answered proposals on a rotation (approve, approve, decline, decide later), then answered anything put off from the board. 9 proposals: 7 approved (two of them after "Decide later"), 2 declined.
  - **Building.** At 08:30 I granted every open ask near the asker's home with the building it named, or the obvious one (flower bed or hedge for green, a bench to sit, garden for food). For "another place to gather" my script built a well, since the tray files the well under Gathering (more on this below). 25 placements: a bakery, two flower beds, a hedge, two benches, Ada's orchard, a granary, Juniper's glasshouse, Wren's banner, the fountain on day 15, three gardens, two jetties and nine wells. One refusal: the glasshouse on day 6 for want of timber.
  - **Talking.** At noon I talked to three people, two questions each, rotating how, mind, hope, me and "What do you think of…" (people and places in turn). I replied to most answers: sorry when offered, otherwise agree and disagree in turn, and "What's been the best of it?" every other time it came up. I left about one answer in nine unanswered. That came to 196 answers and 175 replies: 71 agree, 90 disagree (29 of them "What's been the best of it?") and 14 sorry. On days 12 and 28 I asked everyone "What do you think of me?".
  - **Favours.** One a day at 12:40, 30 in all: mostly timber, some catch, garden, "Look in on…" and "Make peace with…".
  - **Save and reload** on day 10: I closed the page and opened the plain URL.
  - **Losses.** On day 13 I removed the place residents' beliefs held most dear, the commons (held dear by Bram, Juniper, Marlow and Wren), and talked to them 30 minutes later. On day 14 I removed Fen's workplace, the fishing jetty, and talked to Fen 30 minutes later. I talked to them again on days 16 and 17, three days after each loss.
  - **Larder.** From day 15 to day 21 I granted no food asks and on day 15 removed the garden plots (the workplace of Ada and Wren). I rescued it on day 22 with two gardens and a jetty.
  - **Records and survey.** I recorded every resident's mood and standing, food, timber and the granary each night, sampled the speech bubbles each evening, and on the morning of day 31 read the You tab, all six About pages, Goals, Folk and the log in all three filters.
- **Amends check, 13 days, desktop, `bakery` seed 20, no intro.** Because the engaged game never offered me a sorry on the day of a loss, I played a short second game to test it directly. On day 8 I removed the commons (held dear by Bram, Fen and Marlow) and asked each of them me, how and mind at 20 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours afterwards. On day 9 I removed Juniper's workshop and did the same at 20 minutes and 4 hours. On day 10 I chose "Let me explain" wherever it was offered, and on days 11 to 13 sorry.
- **Neglected, 21 days, desktop, `quiet` seed 16, with the intro.** I never built, talked or answered. Seven proposals got "Decide later" because the modal blocks the screen. I read the board on days 1, 4, 7, 10, 14, 17 and 21, recorded every night, and read the You tab on day 21. On day 22 I read every About page and asked everyone how, me, mind and hope, replying sorry or explain where offered and disagree otherwise.
- **Fresh on a phone, 390x844 touch, `bakery` seed 17, with the intro.**
  - Three intro cards and Begin, then 90 real seconds at the starting speed, then all five tabs.
  - Wren from the Folk album: her page, then Talk with four questions and a reply to each.
  - A tap on Bram in the town for the quick card, then its Favour button and "Cut timber".
  - A bench placed by touch with the outline and "✓ Place".
  - Night from 19:00 on day 1 to 06:30 on day 2, then a turn to landscape and back.
- **Headless.** `npm run radio` for `bakery` 18 (30 days, considerate) and `quiet` 19 (30 days), and `npm run inspect` for `bakery` 18.

**Crashes and console.** Nothing crashed and there were no page errors. The console showed only the three.js "PCFSoftShadowMap has been removed" warning and a failed Google font request (the sandbox proxy's certificate, not the game).

**Save and reload worked.** The game came back at the same moment (tick 13721 to 13722) with the same 29 buildings, the log full (514 rows) and no intro (d10-reloaded). At 390 px the page never scrolled sideways (scrollWidth 390 on every check, in portrait, in landscape at 844 and back).

## Harness limits

- **Time.** Software rendering runs at about 1 fps, so I advanced time with `runTicks` and read bubbles and toasts at moments I chose, with the game paused. I could not judge pacing at live speed beyond the phone's 90 seconds.
- **Camera.** To bring a tile on screen I used the debug `focus` call, which does what the board's "Show me" button does. Placement itself was always a click on the map.
- **Rotations, not taste.** Questions, replies, favours and proposal answers followed fixed rotations. My favour rotation landed on only three people (Juniper, Wren and Bram), so they were often over-asked; a real player would spread favours wider.
- **The eight wells are my script's doing.** It answered Fen's "another place to gather" with a well every morning. A real player would try something else after one or two. I count it only as evidence that a well from the Gathering group does not answer that ask, and that the game lets you build a row of wells without comment.
- **No cottage.** I never built a cottage, and the engaged town had no newcomers in 30 days. I note it, but it was my choice as much as the game's.
- **One aborted run.** My first engaged run lost days 7 to 11 to a bug in my own placement script (it did not know the banner's size). I fixed it and replayed from day 1; every engaged number here is from the second run.
- **Timings.** Up to three browser sessions shared four cores, so load times (2.4 to 3.0 s to the debug handle, 6.2 s for the reload) are slower than a real device and I do not score them.

## Scores

### 1. Fun: 6/10

What works, and works better than in review 7:
- **Taking things away now costs you, and you can watch it.** Marlow's standing fell from 0.48 (day 9) to −0.13 (day 16) after I removed the commons, Fen's from 0.52 to 0.21 the day after his jetty went, Wren's from 0.81 (day 12) to 0.39 (day 16). Mean mood dipped from 0.80 (day 12) to 0.70 (day 15). In the amends game Juniper fell from 0.30 to 0.05 the day after I took her workshop, and Marlow to −0.27.
- **Neglect bites.** In the neglected town mean mood fell from 0.72 to 0.55. Bram and Wren were "thinking of leaving" from day 12 and left on days 19 and 20. Ada "has stopped asking you for anything. 'The steward has made their position clear enough.'" (n21-board).
- **The first fortnight is busy and surprising.** Asks, wishes, proposals and favours fill every morning. The trade cart, festivals, gifts on my step ("Ada leaves a parcel of food and timber on your step."), Ada learning to fish from Fen ("Fen nods once, which from Fen is a speech."), Juniper's glasshouse "feeding the town through winter", Marlow letting the cart go ("Turns out I live here.").

It stays at 6 because the second half of a month has nothing left to decide:
- **The board empties.** From day 23 the engaged board said "Nobody is asking for anything just now" on 5 of the last 8 mornings, and "A quiet night. Nothing needs you." on days 24, 28 and 30 (d28-board, d30-board).
- **Food is never a decision.** The granary passed 180 by day 17 and held 251 on the first morning of winter: "Nobody will go hungry this winter." My famine week did not register. With the garden plots and the jetty gone, the larder held between 17 and 21 food every night of days 15 to 21. Its lowest was 10.4 on day 24, two days after I had rebuilt.
- **Timber piles up again.** Timber climbed to 60.6 by day 30 with nothing I wanted to buy. The fountain was open at Hamlet and I built it on day 15; after that the only new building was the chicken coop.
- **Dreams converge.** On days 25 to 29 Juniper, Marlow and Wren all had the same dream, "share the old oak with a friend", with the same next step, "spend some quiet hours at the old oak". Ada, Bram, Fen and Wren all had "make something for" someone.
- **The valley is the same every time.** Four seeds gave the same map, the same six founders and the same opening asks: Bram's bakery, and green for Ada, Fen and Wren (d01-board, n01-board, p02-after-begin).

An hour in I wanted to see who would forgive me. I did not want a second hour of the town itself.

It stays at 7 because a sorry still buys almost anything:

### 5. Expressive and interactive: 7/10

Talking is still the best thing in the game, and it wears better than in review 7:
- **The voices are distinct and a pleasure.**
  - Juniper: "The trees and I have an understanding."
  - Bram: "Ooh, a debate! Wrong, but a debate!"
  - Fen: "Not settling. Resting."
  - Ada: "You may demur. I shall not change my mind."
  - Marlow: "As I told you on day 17: I'd love to decide…"
- **Stock clauses are much rarer.** In 196 answers "Do you know, I still smile about it" came 0 times (10 in review 7), "I like you. You listen." once (7), "What matters to me is" twice (8) and "Nothing lifts me like" twice (8).
- **Responses vary.** 131 of 174 responses were distinct, and none came more than three times.
- **"What's been the best of it?" mostly works.** It brought out things from the week ("The best of it was this: you planted my sister's orchard.", "Mm. You built my glasshouse.").
- **Explain is offered for decisions and can land.** "Let me explain why I said no to your idea." got "Ohhh! Well, why didn't you say!" from Bram.

It stays at 7 because you cannot always say the thing you mean, and some answers don't fit:
- **You cannot apologise straight away.** In the amends game I asked Bram, Fen and Marlow 27 questions in the six hours after I removed the commons. No answer raised it and no chip offered a sorry. The same held for Fen 30 minutes after losing his jetty and for Juniper after losing her workshop. The chip first appears when they raise it themselves, one to three days later. So the round 7 "Not yet." for an early sorry never had a chance to show.
- **"Best of it" leans on one template.** 8 of 29 answers were "the whole valley turned out for…", and three were activity labels ("Reminisce with Wren.").
- **Chips that read a grievance as an opinion.** Fen's "We lost the first fishing jetty, last summer. Rankles." was offered only "That's fair, about the first fishing jetty." and "I don't see the first fishing jetty that way.", with no sorry chip.
- **Agreement with a non-opinion.** Bram: "The fishing jetty? Eh! Haven't decided!" took my one chip, "That's fair, about the fishing jetty.", with "Finally, someone agrees with me!".
- **The same apology, again and again.** Bram tried to apologise to Ada five times in 30 days, "Ada! I was a fool before. Forgive me?" word for word on days 5 and 24.

## Verdict

**The game does not meet the bar.** No count reaches 8: Fun 6, Polished 7, Thinking 7, Opinionated 7, Expressive 7.

Round 7 moved the game forward:
- Losses now cost real standing, for about a week.
- Low trust refuses a sorry.
- Marlow's and Bram's story beats agree with themselves.
- Stock clauses have mostly gone from talk.
- The phone is clean.

What holds the game back now is the second half of a month. The economy is solved by mid-game, the board has nothing to ask, and the dreams converge. A sorry still repairs nearly everything in a day. And a handful of raw or mismatched lines still show up in the conversation panel, the most-read place in the game.

## Problems, ranked by how much they hold the game back

### 1. After the first fortnight there is nothing to decide

What I saw:
- The engaged board read "Nobody is asking for anything just now" on 5 of the last 8 mornings and "A quiet night. Nothing needs you." on three of them.
- The granary held 251 at winter; timber reached 60.6. Removing the garden plots and the jetty never brought the larder below 17 during my famine week.
- The engaged town never grew: 6 people for 30 days. The tier cards say "Up to 12 can make their home here", but nothing told me a cottage had to stand empty first.
- Three residents shared one dream ("share the old oak with a friend"), and four had a "make something for" dream.

Where:
- The winter stores goal ends once the target is met. The surplus is shared out in spring rather than drawn down (the stores events in `src/narrate/narrator.ts` around line 758 tell the story).
- Asks come only from unmet needs, so a well-run town runs out of them.
- Newcomers need an empty home that has stood a while (`newcomers()` in `src/sim/sim.ts` around line 726). The tier card (`celebrateTier` in `web/src/ui/ui.ts`) does not say so.
- The later dreams are drawn from a small pool of "share a place" and "make a gift" templates (`src/sim/story/dreams.ts`).

Fix:
- Give the second fortnight its own pressure: winter appetite or spoilage that draws the granary down, upkeep on buildings, or a second-tier stores goal.
- Have residents ask for things a good town lacks (a cottage for a cousin, a workshop for a newcomer's trade) rather than only for unmet needs.
- Tell the player that a newcomer needs an empty cottage, on the tier card and as an ask ("Rosa's cousin is looking for a cottage").
- Keep any two residents' current dreams different, and weight later dreams by each person's values and tastes.

### 2. A sorry still repairs almost anything, and never on the day

What I saw:
- 13 of 14 sorries were accepted in the engaged game and 7 of 7 in the amends game. Each one raised standing by 0.06.
- Marlow at −0.13 forgave a sorry right after saying "you choose badly".
- Apologising to Juniper for approving Bram's supper worked while Bram stayed delighted.
- All six "think the world of you" on day 30.
- No sorry chip was offered on the day of a loss: 27 questions in six hours after the commons went, and none after the jetty or the workshop.

Where:
- `case 'sorry'` in `src/sim/replies.ts` (around lines 285 to 310) refuses only below −0.5 affinity, or for `destroyed_place` within `TOO_SOON`. Every other sorry gives +0.06 affinity and softens every grievance.
- A sorry chip exists only for a grievance the resident has just raised in an answer.

Fix:
- Offer "I'm sorry I took away the commons" as a chip on any answer from someone hurt by a loss in the last day, so the round 7 "Not yet." can actually happen.
- Make a sorry for a decision that pleased others cost something with those others, or let it be refused as "You'd do it again."
- Scale the gain by standing, so a sorry from below zero earns "We'll see" and a little familiarity, not +0.06.
- Cap the You-tab wording at "thinks well of you" while a loss from the last fortnight is open.

### 3. Raw and mismatched lines in the talk panel

What I saw:
- Stale reply chips under favour answers (d-stale-chips).
- Activity labels as "best of it" answers ("Tease with Juniper!", "Reminisce with Wren.", "Chat with Fen!"), and "A lovely spot." with no place named.
- "As I told you on day 8: I'm well, thank you."
- "whether they want to learn to fish" about Ada, "pip wants to stop the night", "asking you get me nowhere", "Just... Show me.", "Kind of, of course!".
- A grievance offered only opinion chips; "Finally, someone agrees with me!" about something Bram hadn't decided.

Where:
- **Stale chips.** `refreshTalkPanel` in `web/src/ui/ui.ts` (around line 1890) opens `la.offers` whenever the last line is theirs and `lastAnswer` is unreplied. A favour answer leaves the old `lastAnswer` in place.
- **Activity labels.** `bestOfWeek` in `src/narrate/narrator.ts` (line 1085) passes social episodes through `memoryClause`, which returns the episode's raw note for aspects it doesn't phrase.
- **"As I told you".** It is prefixed to any repeated answer, stock pleasantries included (`src/narrate/narrator.ts` line 1260).
- **The fishing line.** `'fen:student'` in `src/content/story.ts` line 127 hard-codes "they".

Fix:
- Clear or mark replied the last answer when a favour or a new question is asked.
- Leave social episodes out of "best of it", or phrase them ("our chat by the brook").
- Use "As I told you" only for hopes and views, not for "I'm well".
- Use the partner's pronouns in `fen:student`.
- Capitalise names after a lead-in, and keep one tic per sentence and none before "of course".
- Offer sorry, not opinion chips, when a "mind" answer is a loss.

### 4. Minds that blame the wrong thing

What I saw:
- Bram, woken by his own approved night of baking, holds "put the bakery there" against me.
- Ada, the morning after losing her workplace: "Quite well, thank you."
- Opposite opinions forming side by side on the same page.
- Wren says "No big plans" as the toast reveals her banner dream.

Where:
- The `disturbed_sleep` branch in `src/sim/sim.ts` (lines 1063 to 1079) raises `spoils_town` against the steward whenever a steward-placed building is loud at night. That holds even when the sleeper works there, asked for it, or the noise is an event the steward approved for them.
- Forming beliefs are listed without checking for an opposite settled or forming one.

Fix:
- Skip the steward grievance when the sleeper works at the noisy building, asked for it, or the noise is an approved event. Let Bram's line be his own ("Baked all night. Worth it.").
- Make losing one's own workplace colour the next "How are you?" ("Lost. The garden plots were my days.").
- Drop a forming belief when its opposite is already held.
- Reveal a dream fact only while the dream is current.

### 5. Asks and the Build tray don't explain each other

What I saw:
- Fen's "another place to gather" stayed open from day 6 to day 13 through eight wells built beside his tent. The well sits in the tray's "Gathering" group; the card's hint says "a bench, or a teahouse".
- The game let me build eight wells in a row and called them "the sixth well" and "the last well".
- On the phone the placement bar stayed up after I left Build.

Where: the "place to gather" ask is met by gathering places with seats. `BUILD_MENU` in `web/src/ui/ui.ts` (line 35) puts the well beside the teahouse and commons.

Fix:
- Move the well out of Gathering, or let it count.
- Have the tray mark which cards would answer an open ask ("Fen would count this").
- Name repeats by place ("the well by Fen's tent").
- Cancel the build tool when another tab is chosen.

### 6. Repetition over a month

What I saw:
- "The whole valley turned out for…" in 8 of 29 "best of it" answers.
- Bram's word-for-word apology to Ada, twice, out of five tries.
- Three identical dreams.
- "Favours asked this week: 3 (that is a lot)." under every favour from day 7 to day 30. That one was my rotation's fault, but the line never varied.

Fix:
- Rest a "best of it" template for a week per resident, as round 7 did for memory openers.
- Let a refused reconciliation wait longer before the next try, and vary its words.
- Tie later dreams to each resident's values so they differ.
